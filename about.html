LEGACY OF SARVEKANKSHAM

Committed to Justice, Guided by Integrity.

SARVEKANKSHAM Law Associates is a progressive, Multi-service Pan India law firm based in Patna, Bihar, dedicated to delivering accessible, high-quality legal counsel and representation. Sarvekanksham is driven by a philosophy of comprehensive welfare and justice for all, the firm stands out for its strong commitment to both rigorous professional litigation and public interest advocacy. The firm maintains a robust presence before the Apex Court, Patna High Court and subordinate courts, balancing traditional legal practice with active digital legal awareness.

Our work

Core Areas of Practice & Expertise

The firm maintains a robust presence before the Apex Court, Patna High Court and subordinate courts, balancing traditional legal practice with active digital legal awareness.

01

Constitutional & Public Interest Litigation (PIL)

Deeply invested in Indian constitutional law, frequently handling matters related to fundamental rights (Article 32 and Article 226), the Basic Structure Doctrine, and judicial review.

Grassroots impact

Champions grassroots public causes, including initiatives to legally protect and regulate the livelihoods of vulnerable communities, including local orchestra and musical performers.

02

Modern Criminal Statutes

The firm actively navigates and provides counsel on India’s modern criminal legal framework, including the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA).

Areas of focus

Anticipatory bails, regular bails, trial defense, forensic evaluation, and FIR quashing petitions.

03

Specialized Civil & Regulatory Law

Expertise spanning crucial socio-legal and local regulatory matters, including:

  • The Bihar Land Encroachment Act and property disputes.
  • Environmental and civic regulations, such as noise pollution and DJ ban compliance.
  • Contemporary civil liabilities, including pet owner liability acts.

What guides us

Key Pillars & Philosophy

Pro Bono & Accessible Justice

A defining feature of Sarvekanksham Law Associates is its commitment to public service, actively offering free legal consultancy to ensure that financial constraints do not act as a barrier to justice.

Dynamic Leadership

The firm is led by dynamic legal professionals, including Advocate Sarvesh Kashyap (Managing Director) and Advocate Kumari Akanksha Rai, combining sharp courtroom litigation with strategic legal consulting.

Legal Journalism & Digital Outreach

Moving beyond the courtroom, the firm is highly proactive in digital legal literacy. They run consistent public awareness campaigns via social media, explanatory videos, and visual guides to break down complex statutory updates—such as major annual tax regime changes—into digestible insights for the general public.

Judicial Transparency & Structural Reform

The firm regularly monitors and addresses structural developments within the Indian judiciary, advocating for transparency against issues like the “Uncle Judges” syndrome and supporting proactive judicial interventions.
