Frequently Asked Questions

More information about arranging an appointment with the firm.

How do I schedule an in-person legal consultation in Patna?

In-person consultations at our Patna chambers are scheduled upon prior request. You can submit your inquiry through our online consultation form or call our office desk directly (+91-9934021776) to schedule a mutually convenient appointment time.

Do you provide online or video consultations for clients outside Bihar?

Yes. We offer remote consultations via secure video conferencing for clients across India, non-resident Biharis, and commercial entities requiring preliminary legal advice or document review.

What documents should I prepare before contacting the firm?

Please have relevant primary legal documents ready: copy of the FIR or complaint for criminal matters, revenue records and title deeds for property disputes, or contracts and correspondence for commercial litigation.
