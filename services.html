Criminal law

Criminal Law & Defence Practice

Navigating complex criminal trials under BNS, BNSS, BSA, bail applications, and quashing petitions.

A. Bail & Pre-Trial Adjudication

  • Anticipatory Bail: Securing pre-arrest bail under the BNSS for clients apprehending arrest in non-bailable offences.
  • Regular & Interim Bail: Moving bail applications before Magistrates, Sessions Courts, High Courts, and the Supreme Court.
  • Quashing Petitions: Filing petitions before the High Court for quashing frivolous, malicious, or politically motivated FIRs and criminal complaints, including learned trial court orders arising from malicious FIRs.

B. Trial Advocacy & Criminal Defence

  • Defence Representation: Representing accused persons across BNS offences, from offences against the human body (including hurt and culpable homicide) to offences against property (including theft, criminal breach of trust, and mischief).
  • Cross-Examination & Evidence Strategy: Evaluating and challenging electronic and physical forensics under the modern provisions of the BSA.
  • Matrimonial & Domestic Violence Crimes: Handling sensitive cases involving dowry allegations, domestic violence complaints, and allegations of sexual offences.

C. White-Collar & Economic Offences

  • Financial Fraud & Cheating: Defending or prosecuting cases concerning criminal breach of trust, forgery, and cheating.
  • Specialized Statutes: Litigation involving the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), the Prevention of Corruption Act (PCA), and cheque bounce matters under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act.
  • Cybercrime Defence: Representing clients in data breaches, identity theft, financial phishing, and cyber-defamation matters involving the Information Technology (IT) Act.

Helpful information

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about these practice areas. Information on this page is general and not legal advice.

How does the firm handle bail under the new Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)?

Our criminal defense team prepares and moves both anticipatory bail and regular bail applications under the procedural mandates of the BNSS before Sessions Courts, the Patna High Court, and the Supreme Court.

Can an FIR be quashed in the Patna High Court?

Yes. If an FIR or criminal complaint is frivolous, malicious, or does not disclose a prima facie cognizable offense, our advocates file quashing petitions before the Patna High Court under the inherent powers of the High Court and corresponding BNSS provisions.

What legal remedies are available in local land encroachment matters?

The practice includes litigation regarding applicable state land enactments, land encroachment regulations, tenancy disputes, and public land eviction matters. Available remedies depend on the relevant law and case circumstances.
